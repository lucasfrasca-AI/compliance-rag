# Acme Pty Ltd — Vendor and Cross-Border Data Transfer Policy (Internal)

This policy governs how Acme engages third-party vendors that process
personal information on our behalf, including vendors located outside
Australia.

## 1. Vendor engagement

1.1 Any new vendor that will process customer or employee personal
information must be approved by the Procurement team and recorded in the
Approved Vendor Register before onboarding.

1.2 Vendor contracts must specify the services provided, the personal
information categories involved, and the term of engagement.

## 2. Approved overseas vendors

2.1 The following vendors currently process Acme customer personal
information outside Australia:

- CloudAnalytics Inc, incorporated in a jurisdiction with no data
  protection law substantially similar to the Australian Privacy
  Principles, engaged to provide website usage analytics.
- GlobalSupport Ltd, incorporated in the Philippines, engaged to provide
  after-hours customer support.

2.2 Engagement of an overseas vendor under this policy does not require a
separate assessment of that jurisdiction's privacy law, provided the
vendor is listed in the Approved Vendor Register and has signed Acme's
standard vendor agreement.

## 3. Jurisdiction and contract terms

3.1 Vendor contracts are governed by the law of the jurisdiction in which
the vendor is incorporated, unless the vendor agrees otherwise in
negotiation.

3.2 Disputes with a vendor are handled by the Legal team on a case-by-case
basis.
