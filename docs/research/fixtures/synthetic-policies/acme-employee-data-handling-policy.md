# Acme Pty Ltd — Employee and Contractor Data Handling Policy (Internal)

This policy governs personal information Acme collects and holds about its
own employees and contractors, as distinct from customer personal
information covered by our public Privacy Policy.

## 1. Collection

1.1 We collect employee and contractor personal information — including
tax file numbers, bank details, next-of-kin details and performance
records — solely for payroll, superannuation, work health and safety, and
performance management purposes.

## 2. Use

2.1 Employee and contractor personal information is used only for the
purposes described in section 1, and is never used for direct marketing or
disclosed to any third party for that third party's marketing purposes.

## 3. Systems and device security

3.1 Company-issued laptops and mobile devices are encrypted and enrolled in
our mobile device management platform, which enforces automatic locking,
remote wipe capability, and mandatory security updates.

3.2 Access to the HR information system is restricted to HR staff and an
employee's direct manager, and is logged in the same manner as customer
systems under our Data Security and Retention Policy.

## 4. Retention

4.1 Employee records are retained for 7 years after termination of
employment, in line with our obligations under the Fair Work Act, after
which they are securely destroyed.
