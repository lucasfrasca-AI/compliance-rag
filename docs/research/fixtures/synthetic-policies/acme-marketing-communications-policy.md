# Acme Pty Ltd — Marketing and Direct Communications Policy (Internal)

This policy governs how the Marketing team uses customer personal
information collected through our website and app for direct
communications.

## 1. Use of collected data for marketing

1.1 Personal information collected at account creation or checkout —
including name, email address and purchase history — may be used by the
Marketing team to send product updates, promotions and personalised offers
by email and SMS, unless the customer has opted out.

1.2 Every marketing communication includes a clear and functional
unsubscribe or opt-out mechanism, actioned within 5 business days of a
customer's request.

## 2. Segmentation and personalisation

2.1 The Marketing team may combine purchase history with website browsing
behaviour to build customer segments for targeted campaigns.

## 3. Campaign vendors

3.1 The Marketing team uses a third-party email delivery platform to send
campaigns. Customer email addresses and first names are shared with this
platform solely to deliver the campaign.
