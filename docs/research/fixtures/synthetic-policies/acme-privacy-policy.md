# Acme Pty Ltd — Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Acme Pty Ltd ("Acme", "we", "us") collects, holds,
uses and discloses personal information, and how you can access or correct
it. This policy is reviewed annually by our Privacy Officer and the current
version is always available on our website and on request, free of charge.

## 1. About this policy

1.1 This policy applies to personal information collected through our
website, our mobile app, our call centre, and in the course of our retail
operations in Australia.

1.2 If you have a complaint about how we have handled your personal
information, you can contact our Privacy Officer at
privacy@acme.example.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 5
business days and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

## 2. What we collect and why

2.1 When you create an account, we collect your name, email address,
postal address, phone number and date of birth, to create and administer
your account and to provide you with the products and services you order.

2.2 When you make a purchase, we collect payment details (processed by our
payment provider, not stored by us) and purchase history, to process your
order and for our own records under tax law.

2.3 At the point we collect personal information — at account creation and
at checkout — we display a notice describing what we collect, why, and who
we may disclose it to, with a link to this policy.

## 3. How we use and disclose your information

3.1 We use your personal information to provide the products and services
you have requested, to respond to your enquiries, and to comply with our
legal obligations.

3.2 We do not use your personal information for direct marketing, and we
do not disclose it to any third party for that third party's own direct
marketing, unless you have given us your opt-in consent. You may withdraw
this consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing
message or by contacting us.

3.3 We disclose personal information to our logistics partners, our
payment provider, and our IT service providers, solely to the extent
necessary for them to provide services to us.

## 4. Overseas disclosure

4.1 Some of our service providers, including trusted global service
providers who support our IT systems, are located outside Australia. We
take reasonable steps to ensure any overseas recipient of your personal
information handles it consistently with the Australian Privacy
Principles.

4.2 A current list of the countries in which our overseas service
providers are located is available on request from our Privacy Officer.

## 5. Security

5.1 We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold
from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or
disclosure. Further detail on our technical and organisational controls is
set out in our internal Data Security and Retention Policy.

## 6. Access and correction

6.1 You may request access to the personal information we hold about you,
and request that we correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete,
irrelevant or misleading. Our process for handling these requests is set
out in our Access and Correction Request Procedure.

6.2 We will not charge you a fee for making a request for access or
correction.
