# Acme Pty Ltd — Access and Correction Request Procedure (Internal)

This procedure sets out how staff must handle a customer's request to
access or correct their personal information, referenced by section 6 of
our public Privacy Policy.

## 1. Receiving a request

1.1 A request may be made by email, phone, or through the "My Account"
page. Staff receiving a request must log it in the Request Register the
same day.

1.2 Staff must verify the requester's identity using our standard identity
verification checklist before actioning the request.

1.3 Where a customer disputes what they were told about our collection of
their personal information, staff should refer to the notice shown to the
customer at signup or checkout at the relevant time. This procedure does
not specify what that notice must contain, how to determine which version
of the notice applied on a given date, or how a dispute about its content
should be resolved.

## 2. Access requests

2.1 Once identity is verified, the assigned staff member compiles the
personal information held about the customer and provides it in the form
requested (typically a PDF export), as soon as practicable after the
request is verified.

2.2 If a request is refused in whole or in part — for example, where
providing access would unreasonably impact the privacy of another
individual — the customer is given written reasons for the refusal and
information about how to complain.

## 3. Correction requests

3.1 Once identity is verified and the correction is confirmed accurate,
the assigned staff member updates the customer's record and notifies any
third party to whom the incorrect information was previously disclosed,
where practicable.

3.2 If Acme is not satisfied a correction is warranted, the customer may
ask us to associate a statement with the record noting that the customer
considers the information inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant
or misleading.

## 4. Escalation

4.1 A request outstanding for more than 10 business days without a
response sent to the customer must be escalated to the Privacy Officer.
