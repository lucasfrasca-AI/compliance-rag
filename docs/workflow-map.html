Compliance RAG — how a request moves through the system

Hybrid graph RAG on GCP with Qdrant and Neo4j. Two loops: the corpus loop keeps law and policy current; the assessment loop answers questions and checks documents. Costs are monthly estimates at ~2,000 queries and a ~30 MB corpus, single tenant.
