Policies

Your workspace's own policies, with every version of each. A new version is chosen here, by the policy it is for, never matched by title: two policies may share a title. It takes effect today unless you pick a date. A future date leaves the version it replaces in force until then; a past date needs a reason, recorded with the version. Withdrawing keeps the text, so earlier citations still open. Purging deletes it, and asks you to type how many findings cite it.
