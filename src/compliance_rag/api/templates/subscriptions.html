Subscriptions

The library is the law and standards every workspace shares. A subscription is this workspace's tick against one instrument: answers and checks read the instruments you tick, and nothing else from the library. Unsubscribing removes the tick only. The instrument stays in the library, no other workspace sees a change, and earlier citations still open.
