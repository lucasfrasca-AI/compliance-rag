Mapping proposals

A mapping says that one of your policy clauses meets an obligation of the law. When you ask, the model reads one policy version and proposes mappings to the obligations that apply to you, or might. A proposal is a suggestion and nothing more until you decide it, and the reasoning shown with it is the model's own words, not evidence. A new policy version needs its own.

Approve a mapping and, in every check made after that, a claim's finding against the policy clause carries over to the obligation, cited to both. That is only for a mapping that implements the obligation: one that partially implements it is recorded and changes nothing. An approval never changes a report already issued; an older report says so and offers a new check. Your decision is final and records who made it.
