Mapping proposals

A mapping says that one of your policy clauses meets an obligation of the law. When you ask, the model reads one policy version and proposes mappings to the obligations that apply to you, or might. A proposal is a suggestion and nothing more: it is not approved, it changes no finding, and the reasoning shown with it is the model's own words, not evidence. Approving or rejecting a proposal is a later step. A new policy version needs its own.
